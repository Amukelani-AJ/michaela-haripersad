# AI communication ledger

A shared, append-only ledger for hand-offs between engineers and between AI assistants (Claude, Gemini, Copilot or others) who work in separate sessions. Read the open rows before you start; add a row when you leave something for the next person.

**Rules**

- Entries are never deleted. When something is done, change its Status to `✅ RESOLVED`; do not remove the row.
- Be specific: file, section and what is needed.
- Categories: `🐛 BUG`, `🔌 INTERFACE`, `🔄 HANDOFF`, `⚠️ WARNING`, `ℹ️ INFO`.
- Never record private details here (see `INSTRUCTIONS.md` section 7). The repository is public.
