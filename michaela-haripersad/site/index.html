01 Artist biography

My story

Before anyone knew my name, there was simply a girl from Johannesburg with a dream too big to ignore.

I’m Michaela Haripersad, a 22-year-old singer who believes music has the power to comfort, inspire, and remind people they’re never alone.

I’ve learned that dreams aren’t measured by how quickly they happen, but by the courage it takes to keep chasing them.

Every cover I’ve recorded, every competition I’ve entered, and every opportunity I’ve embraced has been another chapter in a story I’m still writing.

Maybe this biography will one day become a memory. Maybe my future self will read these words from a studio, a stage, or somewhere I once only imagined.

If you are reading this, I hope you remember the girl who dared to believe before she had proof, who trusted God’s timing over her own, and who refused to let fear have the final word.

I don’t dream of being known simply for singing; I dream of leaving people with hope. If my voice can make one person feel seen, healed, or inspired, then every step of this journey will have been worth it.

This isn’t my arrival. It’s my beginning.
